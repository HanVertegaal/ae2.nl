Choose your device

Open a device card on Devices to reach its control page. Each guide below describes the controls that DIRAC Field presents for that device. Shared project, measurement and export operations are linked rather than repeated.

GuideWhat it covers
AetherSLM parameter selection and Start/Stop; signal recording; Identify; microphone calibration; Aether-specific settings and recovery actions.
Echo and AtlasSignal configuration, playback and source-triggered measurement. Echo's calibrated levels and Atlas's gain controls are distinguished.
HBK 2755White, Pink and User buttons, gain, stored user signals and measurement capture.
HBK 2255Recording duration, capture and retrieval, input-level indication and source-measurement eligibility.

Shared page elements

The top app bar identifies the device and gives access to the app menu. Where reported, battery and connection indications help you check readiness. Wait for connection or initialization to finish before issuing commands.

Recording and source-measurement pages show a Save to destination. This is a project/group in the app. It is not the equipment's storage card or network folder. A progress overlay can cover the page while acquisition, retrieval or processing is running.

Use menu → Settings from a device page for its settings. Available sections depend on the device. The common controls are described in Device Settings and Maintenance; the individual guides explain the device-specific additions.

Controls and recorded results

A source control page sets what the source plays. A receiver control page controls acquisition. After a capture, use Projects to inspect the stored result. Inspecting stored data does not require starting the original sound source again.

The playback button in a saved measurement's detail page plays stored audio through the phone or tablet. It is separate from remote source playback.
