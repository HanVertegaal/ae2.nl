Power sources and wake-up

Aether is normally used wirelessly on its internal battery, without a power adapter. External power is available only through the USB-C connector, which can power Aether and charge its battery. Use a suitable USB-C supply and cable when charging or external power is needed.

Aether can wake when its orientation-sensitive switch activates or USB-C power is connected. If it does not start, connect USB-C power and check the LED indication.

Power-off and standby

Stop recording, calibration and live measurements before switching off. Automatic power-off limits battery use when Aether is inactive; the timeout is configured through DIRAC Field. The minimum enabled timeout is 5 minutes; zero disables automatic power-off.

On battery power, power-off shuts the device down. With USB-C power connected, the device can enter standby while charging remains controlled. Disconnect USB-C power before requesting a complete battery-powered shutdown from the app.

Charging and low battery

Check the battery state in DIRAC Field before starting a long recording. In charging standby, the LEDs breathe white while charging is enabled. To protect the battery during prolonged connection, standby charging stops at about 80% and resumes below about 75%.

At very low battery voltage, Aether switches off. Finish measurements and retrieve the required files before the battery becomes empty. Do not rely on an interrupted recording as a completed result.

Status indicators

A breathing indication gradually brightens and dims. A flashing indication switches on and off.

IndicationMeaning
Flashing yellowStarting up.
Flashing cyanConnecting to a Wi-Fi network.
Breathing greenHotspot mode, including idle operation while the normal hotspot is active.
Breathing blueIdle outside hotspot mode. Check the app for connection and measurement status.
Rotating purpleSearching for or measuring the acoustic calibration tone.
Flashing redPre-recording attention period.
Breathing redRecording.
Flashing orangeFirmware update in progress.
Breathing whiteCharging enabled during standby.
Brief multicolour patternIdentify, requested from the controlling application.
Alternating red and whiteService required. See Troubleshooting and Recovery.

The app's LED brightness setting controls ordinary status indications. The pre-recording attention indication uses full brightness even when ordinary status brightness is set to zero. LEDs can also be off during standby when charging is paused, so darkness alone does not establish that the battery is empty.

Attention and Identify

The attention time is a configurable delay before recording, accompanied by flashing red LEDs. It allows people near the microphone to settle before acquisition starts. Setting the attention time to zero removes this delay.

Once the app is connected to Aether, Identify briefly shows a multicolour pattern to help locate the selected device among several Aethers. Identify requires an active connection. It finishes automatically and is separate from the measurement state.
