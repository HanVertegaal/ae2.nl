Microphone interface

ParameterSpecification

Acquisition and storage

ParameterSpecification
Sampling rate48 kHz.
Recorded channelsOne microphone channel.
Recording formatWAV, 32-bit IEEE floating-point samples representing sound pressure in pascals, using the Aether calibration scale.
StorageInternal SD card. Supplied capacity to be confirmed.
Acoustic calibrationStable 1 kHz tone with a user-specified calibrator level.

Power and control

ParameterSpecification
Power sourcesInternal battery for normal wireless use. External power and charging through USB-C only.
Battery capacity and operating timeTo be confirmed.
Control connectionWi-Fi, with Station and Hotspot modes.
Local indicationsRGB LED ring; see status indicators.
OperationDIRAC Field: SLM and recording. DIRAC desktop: recording only, without SLM. Orientation-sensitive wake-up and recovery confirmation.
