Quick Start

This page takes you from an unconnected Aether to a first measurement. Detailed application controls belong in the DIRAC Field guide.

Before you start

  • Aether and its 1/2-inch IEPE measurement microphone, normally supplied together.
  • A charged Aether battery. USB-C power is optional during use.
  • A phone or tablet running DIRAC Field, and the Aether device password.
  • A suitable acoustic calibrator when calibrated sound levels are required.

Prepare Aether

  1. Connect the microphone directly to Aether's BNC input and place the assembly so it will remain undisturbed. No microphone cable is needed.
  2. Wake Aether using its orientation-sensitive switch and wait for the startup indication to finish. Normal use is on battery power; connect a suitable USB-C supply if charging or external power is needed.
  3. Connect the controlling device to the same network as Aether, or to Aether's hotspot. See Remote Control.
  4. Select Aether in DIRAC Field and authenticate with its device password.

After the connection is established, you can use Identify to confirm which physical Aether you have selected. Identify requires an active connection.

Check and calibrate

Check that the live microphone level responds to sound. Allow the microphone power to settle; Aether applies a five-second IEPE warm-up when the supply has just been enabled.

For calibrated measurements, fit the acoustic calibrator, check its selected level and run calibration. See Microphone and Calibration. Remove the calibrator before measuring.

Make a first measurement

In DIRAC Field, select either SLM for sound level measurement or Recording for a signal recording. Choose the required parameter or recording duration and start the operation.

A recording may be preceded by microphone warm-up and a flashing red attention indication. The red breathing indication identifies recording. Wait for completion and check the result in the app before moving the equipment or switching off.

Use DIRAC Field to retrieve and manage recordings. Before leaving the site, check that the required measurements have been saved.

Aether can also record with the desktop version of DIRAC. The desktop application supports recording only, without Aether's SLM function. See the DIRAC guide for desktop recording workflows.

Finish

Stop the active measurement or recording before disconnecting equipment. For battery operation, use the app's power-off action when finished. Aether can also power down automatically after inactivity. See Power and Status Indicators.
